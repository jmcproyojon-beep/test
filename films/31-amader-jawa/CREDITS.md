# CREDITS — আমাদের আর যাওয়া হলো না (31-amader-jawa)

Song «আমাদের আর যাওয়া হলো না»: vocal & lyrics Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah); master supplied by the artist. Edited by Paloan.

## Picture
- Every drawing (characters, places, FX, lettering layout) is generated in code (`cel.js`, `chars.js`, `index.html`): no images or video generation.
- The singer's character design follows his own character-reference sheet (flat cap, curls, goatee, cream tee, khaki trousers).
- Paper and grain textures: the mg-styles-15 shared texture set (`/assets/textures`, CC0).

## Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1, via @fontsource on npm)
- Atma — lyrics. - Galada — hand-lettered titles. - Anek Bangla — fallback.

## Sound
- Music: the artist's own master.
- Paper swish and camera-shutter SFX: synthesised in code with `lib/audio` (mgaudio, MIT).

## Timing
- Lyric timings: Gemini (3.8 / 3.7 / 3.6 flash, 3 flash preview) listening to overlapping 30 s windows; 79 of 84 lines agreed by two or more models.
