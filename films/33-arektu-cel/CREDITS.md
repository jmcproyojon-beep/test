# «আরেকটু থাকো» — cel / line-boil story film
- Song, voice and lyrics: Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah). Edit: Paloan.
- Engine: mg-styles-15 cel / line-boil toolkit (MIT), extended with the room, tea stall, rickshaw, station and train scenes.
- Fonts (SIL OFL): Atma, Galada (Bangla lettering).
- Lyric times: two-model alignment of the master (lines.json).
- Render: link this folder as `mg-styles-15/demos/33-arektu-cel` (scripts/setup-mg-styles.sh does it), put the master at
  `assets/song.mp3`, then `python3 demos/33-arektu-cel/audio.py` and `MG_CPU=1 scripts/mg-render.sh demos/33-arektu-cel` (in chunks).
