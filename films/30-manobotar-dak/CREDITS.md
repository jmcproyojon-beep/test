Reused from `demos/06-collage` (same repository, MIT): the Victorian portrait pieces (Pierre Petit, Met Museum CC0), Hubble deep-space wedge (NASA, PD),
hawk / rose / butterfly / rocket / manicule cut-outs (public-domain engravings and plates) and the kraft / red paper pages.
Textures (`/assets/textures`): grain, dust, paper and washi tape from the repository's shared CC0 texture set.

## Sound

- Music: the artist's own master.
- Paper, scissors, pop, stamp and tape SFX: synthesised in code with `lib/audio` (mgaudio, MIT).

No real brand marks appear on screen: the newspaper «দৈনিক পৃথিবী» and all flags are invented; the T-shirt print in the singer's photo is covered by a paper patch.
