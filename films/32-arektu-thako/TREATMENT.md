# আরেকটু থাকো — treatment (Chalkboard)

**Song:** «আরেকটু থাকো» (Stay a little longer), Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah). 4:04, gentle Bangla pop ballad.
**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, lyric music video. No narration: the song is the voice. The subtitle plate carries each sung
line in Bangla with a free English translation, in and out with the vocal (the style's subtitle rule, with Bangla for Chinese).

## Logline
An empty classroom at dusk, rain on the window, the wall clock running. On the board, two hands talk in chalk —
his in white, hers in pale yellow — and one lesson is taught the way a teacher would: what the heart believes is written,
wiped in one pass, and the truth is written in its place. «সময় থামবে» becomes «সময় থামবে না».

## The lesson (one idea)
*A moment cannot be kept; it can only be lived.* The board asks to stop time and learns it can't.

## Three candidate structures
1. **The word lesson.** One big word, «থাকো» (stay), taught like a literacy lesson: its letters underlined, the accent chalk
   tracks «থাকো» through every chorus at the same height, and the board ends with it alone. Clear, but static over 4 minutes.
2. **Two boards in dialogue.** Left board: his lines (white). Right board: her replies (yellow, the "second hand").
   The verses write the memories as keywords and small chalk drawings (window + rain, a cup, an old photo frame);
   the pre-chorus does the write–wipe–rewrite; the choruses grow one sentence across the seam. Tells the story, uses the room.
3. **The clock lesson.** The wall clock is the subject; each section is "a reading of the time", ending at the hour she leaves.
   Strong metaphor, but the board turns into a timetable and the love story becomes abstract.

**Choice: 2, with the tracked word from 1.** The dialogue carries the story (who is leaving, who asks); «থাকো» in the one
accent chalk (orange) is the component the lesson tracks; the clock on the wall keeps moving in every wide shot (structure 3's
idea, as set dressing only).

## Bangla on the board (engine extension, documented in the project)
The engine draws Chinese from hand-authored stroke data and Latin from a single-line font. Bangla has neither, so each word
the board needs is converted once into centre-line strokes: the word is set in an OFL Bangla face, skeletonised, traced into
polylines, and ordered the way Bangla is handwritten — each letter left to right, its body before its marks, and the মাত্রা
(headline) drawn across the whole word last as one stroke. Every word is checked by eye at film size before shooting.

## Sound
The song is the soundtrack (artist's master). Under it, very quiet chalk foley (one grain chain per stroke, accent chalk duller),
the felt pass of the wipe, the panel's low roll and stop, a distant school bell at the opening. Master −14 LUFS, TP ≤ −1 dBTP.

## Defaults taken
Language Bangla (board + plate), English translation on the plate. No storyboard stop. 30 fps.
