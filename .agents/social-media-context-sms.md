## Target Audience

- **Primary audience**:
  1. NRB investors (Bangladeshis in the Middle East, UK, USA, Malaysia) — want to invest at home but can't visit the site
  2. Dhaka middle/upper-middle class families (30–50, salaried professionals and business owners) buying a first flat
  3. Landowners looking for a trustworthy developer for joint-venture projects
  4. Small investors entering prime areas through land shares and installment plans
- **Pain points**: Fake or disputed land documents (জাল দলিল) and double-selling; delayed handover; hidden costs after booking; uncertainty about registration costs and taxes; poor construction quality discovered after moving in; worry that political instability will stall projects or hurt prices; inflation eroding savings
- **Goals**: A safe, title-clear asset for the family; value growth in prime locations; rental income (especially NRBs); a company that will still be there and answer the phone in 10 years; affordable entry via installments
- **Sophistication level**: Beginner to intermediate — knows the basics of buying property but not the legal/tax details
- **Where they hang out**: Facebook (page posts, Reels, Messenger inquiries); Bangla and English mixed; NRBs often browse in the evening Bangladesh time

## Anti-Patterns

- **Topics to avoid**: Politics or political parties; competitors; religion-based selling; unverified price predictions
- **Tones to avoid**: Pushy/salesy, hype, exaggerated promises
- **Content types to avoid**: Guaranteed-return claims; fake scarcity; stock or AI images presented as real completed projects (label renders as "Artist's impression" / "প্রস্তাবিত ডিজাইন"); designs with misspelled or garbled text (proofread every graphic before posting)
