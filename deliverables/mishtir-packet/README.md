# মিষ্টির প্যাকেট (The Box of Sweets) — 70 s vertical animated short

- `mishtir-packet_9x16.mp4`: 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, 70.0 s, −15.3 LUFS / −3.3 dBTP, final second silent.
- **Production method:** an animated 2.5D vector illustration built in HyperFrames (parallax camera, haze, dust, film grain). It is **not** AI-generated video: on 19 Oct 2026, Veo 3.1, Gemini Omni and the Gemini image models all returned "quota exceeded" on the free plan, and HeyGen/fal were unreachable from this environment.
- **Voices:** Gemini TTS, with a different prebuilt voice per character: Dadu Kak (Algenib, slowed and deepened), Chhana (Puck, raised slightly), Mojid Chacha (Achird). Every line was transcribed and matched the script (0.88–1.00). Beak and mouth movement follows each line's loudness curve.
- **Music and sound:** original synthesized cello drone, piano notes, city ambience, doorbell, clock tick, wind and wing flaps.
- **No on-screen text**, as requested.
- `source/` holds everything needed to regenerate the film: the composition, the generators and the voice manifest.
