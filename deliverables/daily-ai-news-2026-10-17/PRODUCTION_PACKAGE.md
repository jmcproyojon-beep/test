- Original synthesized bed at 104 BPM, A minor → F → C → G (2 bars each).
- Layers: detuned saw pad, sine sub-bass on every beat, soft kick on beats 1 and 3, filtered 8th-note hats, quiet 16th-note pluck arpeggio.

- Sits about 17 dB under the voice, and a sidechain compressor ducks it further whenever the narrator speaks, so it never masks speech.
